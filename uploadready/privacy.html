UploadReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

UploadReady helps users prepare selected photos and PDFs for submission by compressing, resizing, cropping, converting supported image formats, and creating PDF files.

Device-first processing

UploadReady does not upload selected photos or documents to an UploadReady server in the default conversion flow. Basic conversions are processed on the user's device. Files are saved or shared only when the user chooses those actions.

Data accessed by the app

  • User-selected photos and documents used only to create the requested output.
  • Local conversion history, daily export counters, and UI language preference stored on the device.
  • Photo library or document picker access requested only when the user selects files.

Advertising

UploadReady uses Google AdMob banner ads and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional. The app does not ask users to click ads to receive a reward.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or share approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Users can delete local history, locally saved app files, and export counters from the Settings screen. UploadReady does not create user accounts in the first release.

Children

UploadReady is not designed for children and is not directed to children as its primary audience.
