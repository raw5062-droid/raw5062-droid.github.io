Photo Compressor Resizer PDF (UploadReady) Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app listed on Google Play as Photo Compressor Resizer PDF and also known inside the app as UploadReady. The app is published on Google Play by Fortune Atelier and helps users prepare photos and PDFs for submission by compressing, resizing, cropping, converting image formats, and creating PDFs. Basic conversions are processed on the user's device by default.

App and Developer Identity

  • Google Play app name: Photo Compressor Resizer PDF
  • App/internal name: UploadReady
  • Localized store names: 사진 용량 줄이기 크기 줄이기 PDF; 写真圧縮 リサイズ PDF
  • Google Play developer / publisher: Fortune Atelier
  • Developer/legal contact shown on Google Play: 안동인
  • Android package name: com.uploadready.app
  • Privacy contact: ranking.econ@gmail.com

Local Processing

Photo Compressor Resizer PDF (UploadReady) does not upload selected photos or documents to a developer-operated UploadReady or Fortune Atelier server in the default conversion flow. Selected files are used on the device to create the output requested by the user.

Data Accessed by the App

  • Photos and documents selected by the user for conversion.
  • Local conversion history stored on the device.
  • Daily export counters stored on the device.
  • UI language preference stored on the device.
  • Purchase status for ad removal, checked through Google Play on the user's device.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Photo Compressor Resizer PDF (UploadReady) uses Google AdMob banner ads and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional, and users do not need to click an ad to receive the stated reward after viewing.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may process data such as IP address, product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for more information.

Saving, Sharing, Retention, and Deletion

Files are saved locally or shared only when the user taps the save or share button. Users can delete local history, saved app files, and export counters from the Settings screen. Photo Compressor Resizer PDF (UploadReady) does not create user accounts in the first release.

Children

Photo Compressor Resizer PDF (UploadReady) is not designed for children and is not directed to children as its primary audience.
